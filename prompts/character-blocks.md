# Character & Location Prompt Blocks

Copy-paste descriptors. These are the locked traits, already written as prompt text, so nobody
has to paraphrase the bible under time pressure — paraphrasing is where drift starts.

Rule: include the block for **every** character in the panel, in full, every time. Shortening a
block because "the model already knows" is the single most common cause of identity drift.

---

## CHAR-DEEROCK
Reference: `characters/deerock.png`

```
CHAR-DEEROCK — Deerock, 25, male, frontend developer, 172 cm, slim build.
Hair: dark PURPLE-black, medium length, layered, slightly messy — reads purple, not black.
Glasses: ALWAYS, thin dark rectangular frame.
Face: youthful, slim, gentle features. Clean chin. NO beard, NO moustache, NO stubble.
The only facial hair is a very thin, tiny, sparse patch directly beneath the lower lip,
stopping well before the chin.
Expression register: small and contained — slight frown, small smile, flat stare. Never broad.
Accent colour: one small orange detail on his clothing.
```

## Separation clauses

Add the matching clause whenever both characters of a pair are in frame.

```
SEPARATION — Deerock vs Kai:
Deerock has purple-black hair AND glasses, slim, 172 cm, contained expression.
Kai has pure black hair and NO glasses, athletic, 178 cm, open grin, headphones around neck.
They must be instantly distinguishable in silhouette.
```

```
SEPARATION — Aki vs Mika:
Aki's hair is UP in a bun; Mika's hair is DOWN, long and wavy and lighter.
Aki wears black cargo trousers and carries a cream tote; Mika wears plain black wide trousers
and carries a structured shoulder bag. They must not wear the same colour top in one panel.
```

## Location blocks

```
LOC-HOME — reference: locations/home.png
Deerock's house: small single-storey landed house in Pesona Ciputih, Cisauk, unit C12.
Front yard with low planting, carport holding a grey Honda car and a black Honda Vario 150.
Inside: living room with grey sofa and TV, kitchen & dining with open shelving and a wooden
table, Bedroom 1 = Deerock's room with dual-monitor desk, window blinds, plants and a dark bed.
Tidy but lived-in. This is a HOUSE, not an apartment.
```

```
LOC-STATION — reference: locations/krl-station.png
Sentra Lintas Station: elevated KRL station, red `SL` mark, steel-and-concrete structure,
concourse with ticket gates, escalators, Platforms 1 and 2 with numbered blue signs,
tactile paving, yellow safety line, city skyline behind. Signage bilingual Indonesian/English.
```

```
LOC-KRL — reference: locations/krl-interior.png
KRL Commuter carriage, Tanah Abang–Rangkasbitung line. Blue patterned bench seats,

door area with route map, ceiling info display. Believable weekday commuter density.
```

```

NEXA Workspace, mid-high-rise, Jakarta business district. Lobby with the NEXA reception wall,

dual monitors, plants, and fixed desk positions for Deerock, Aki, Kai, Rin and Mika.
Meeting room M1 behind glass. Pantry and lounge common area.
```

```
LOC-CAFE — reference: locations/cafe.png
LUMA Café, South Jakarta business district. Modern, cosy and minimal: concrete and warm wood,

```
PROP-MOTORCYCLE — reference: props/motorcycle-vario150.jpg
Honda Vario 150, model year 2017. Matte dark grey/black bodywork, GOLD wheels.
