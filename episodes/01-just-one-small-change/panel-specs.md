# DEEROCK — Episode 01 Panel Specifications

## Scope and production guardrails

- **Source storyboard:** `episodes/01-just-one-small-change/storyboard.md`. This document expands
  it. It adds no scenes and changes no dialogue.
- **Format:** 5 pages, each a 4:5 portrait page (1080 × 1350). 14 panels: 3 · 3 · 3 · 3 · 2.
  Within a page, read left to right, then top to bottom.
- **Production route:** Route A — each page is generated in one shot
  (`bible/consistency-guide.md` section 7). Output goes to `results/` as `ep01_page01_v001.png` …
  `ep01_page05_v001.png`. If one panel keeps failing, generate only that panel (Route B, e.g.
  `ep01_p03_panel02_v001.png`) and paste it over the page.
- **Density:** every page stays within the series limits — 2–3 panels, at most 2 bubbles per
  panel, at most 5 per page. Per-page counts are listed below.
- **Visual treatment:** as the trailer. Contemporary slice-of-life manga/anime: clean linework,
  restrained cel shading, subtle halftone texture, soft cinematic lighting, realistic modern
  Indonesian environments. Not photorealistic.
- **Text treatment:** only the dialogue, thoughts, SFX and screen text listed per panel. No
  captions, slogans, name tags, role labels or page titles. **The storyboard's page titles —
  "Hari yang tenang", "Ternyata" and so on — are for production only and never appear on a page.**
  Desk nameplates from the office sheet stay out of focus or out of frame.
- **Hard exclusions:** no katana, ninja, samurai, tactical costume, supernatural effects, action
  aura, glowing weapon or cyberpunk elements. No chibi reactions, sweat drops or shock lines —
  surprise in this episode is always a small, human beat.
- **Names:** Deerock is always "Deerock" in full.
