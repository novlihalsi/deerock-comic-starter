# Episode 01 — Just One Small Change

## Format
3 pages, 3–4 panels per page. Prototype episode.

## Opening
Start at Deerock's home in the morning, then KRL commute, then office.

## Page 1 — Morning
1. Deerock wakes up / alarm.
2. Quick preparation.
3. Coffee and simple breakfast.
4. Leaves home with backpack.

Tone: quiet, ordinary, relatable.

## Page 2 — Commute
1. Arrives at KRL station.
2. Inside KRL, Deerock checks team chat.
3. Message snippets from Aki, Kai, Rin, Mika.
4. Deerock notices a new Figma update.

Tone: observational; establish Jakarta/KRL as recurring world.

## Page 3 — Office
1. Arrives at office.
2. Greets team: Aki, Kai, Rin, Mika.
3. Starts work at desk; Figma/code/API/QA checklist establish workflow.
4. Mika messages: "Oh iya, ada satu perubahan kecil lagi..."
End on Deerock's quiet reaction.

## Key requirement
This pilot should introduce the whole team without making the story feel like an exposition dump.

Use the canonical character distinctions in `bible/character-bible.md`, especially Deerock vs Kai.
