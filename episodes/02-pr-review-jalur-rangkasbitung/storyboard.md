## Wardrobe

`D-W01` and `K-W01` throughout. Everyone else is an anonymous commuter.

- **Backpack:** on Deerock's back at the gates; swung to his front on the platform before the doors
  open, and worn there in the carriage (KRL etiquette, `bible/story-world.md`); on his back again in
  the lobby; set down at his desk.
- **Headphones:** Deerock's over-ear headphones are on his head in the carriage (pages 3–5) and
  never around his neck — that is Kai's cue. From the lobby on they are put away; in 8.1 they lie
  on his desk.
- **Lanyard:** none on the train. It appears in the lobby, where he taps his access card.

## Premise

Deerock's commute is a system: the train he catches, the carriage he stands in, the door that
stops in front of the exit escalator, and forty minutes of dozing on his feet. The one thing the
system cannot calculate is the rush hour itself. On the Rangkasbitung line the morning trains
toward Tanah Abang reach Sentra Lintas already full from the stations up the line; boarding means
being pushed in by the crowd behind, and once inside nobody can move — pressed so tight that
Deerock can doze standing up without holding on, but also so tight he can barely lift his phone.

Ten minutes in, Kai messages him: a pull request that has to be merged before nine. Deerock reviews
hundreds of lines of code on a phone screen, one arm pinned, in a packed and swaying carriage. At
the office, Kai thanks him for the fast approval. Deerock would rather not say how it happened —
and if anything breaks in production, he knows exactly who to blame: carriage 3.

## Why this version

- **Rush hour is the antagonist.** The crowd shapes every train beat: it pushes Deerock aboard
  (page 2), holds him upright while he dozes (page 3), pins the arm holding his phone (page 4) and
  jostles him through the review (page 5). The joke is how ordinary this is to him, so he stays
  deadpan throughout, and the crowd is drawn as real rush hour on this line — packed, never
  cartoonish.
- **The math pays off.** Page 1 is titled *Matematika Komuter*, so the numbers Deerock sets up come
  back: the crowd is the variable his math never includes (2.1), the forty minutes of sleep turn
  into ten in the lobby (6.1), and carriage 3 takes the blame in the last line (8.1).
- **"Gerbong 3" is set up before it is used.** The draft planned "pintu 3" on the platform and
  blamed "gerbong 3" at the end. The platform thought now names the carriage, so the final line is
  a callback rather than a new fact.
- **The lobby thought moved to the math.** "Akhirnya… oksigen." would land when he steps off the
  train, but the lobby is some thirty minutes and a walk later. "Rencana tidur 40 menit. Dapetnya
  10." keeps the lobby beat about the morning he just had.
- **The Kai exchange is a shot and reverse shot.** Deerock's line and Kai's "…Oke?" sit in two
  side-by-side panels, so the pause between them is a panel gutter instead of two bubbles in one
  frame.
- **The phone screen goes in an inset.** The screen-facing rule (`bible/visual-style.md`) keeps the
  phone pointed at Deerock; the inset on page 4 shows its message close up without turning it
  toward the camera, and without repeating the over-the-shoulder KRL phone shots of the trailer and
  Episode 01.
- **Deerock in full.** Kai's two "Rock"s are now "Deerock" (`AGENTS.md`, character bible).

About 90 words of dialogue, thought and screen text across eight pages.

## Page 1 — Matematika Komuter
Layout `2-STACK`, 1.1 about 55% of the height. Tone: a routine timed to the minute. This page is
the carousel cover.

**1.1 · `LOC-STATION`, concourse ticket gates · 07:25 · Deerock, anonymous commuters**
Sentra Lintas concourse, the `Ke Platform / To Platform` sign above the gates, a steady stream of
commuters. Deerock taps his e-money card on the gate reader mid-stride, calm and unhurried — a man
running a routine he has done a thousand times. Backpack on his back.
- Thought — Deerock: *07:25. Kereta masuk 07:30. Aman.*

**1.2 · `LOC-STATION`, Platform 1 · 07:29 · Deerock, anonymous commuters**
A crowded platform. Deerock stands just behind the yellow tactile line, precisely where the
carriage door will stop, swinging his backpack round to his front, while the red-and-silver train
pulls in. Commuters pack in behind him.
- Thought — Deerock: *Gerbong 3, pintu tengah. Pas turun, langsung depan eskalator.*

## Page 2 — Jam Berangkat
Layout `2-DIAG`, a gentle slant. Tone: the plan meets rush hour.

**2.1 · `LOC-STATION`, Platform 1 · 07:30 · Deerock, anonymous commuters** — upper piece
The doors slide open exactly in front of Deerock — onto a carriage already packed wall to wall:
backs, shoulders and front-worn backpacks filling the doorway. Deerock looks at it, deadpan.
- Thought — Deerock: *Satu variabel yang nggak pernah masuk hitungan.*

**2.2 · `LOC-KRL`, door area · 07:30 · Deerock, anonymous commuters** — lower piece
The crowd behind surges forward and carries Deerock in, his glasses slightly askew and his face
neutral, as the passengers inside make room that did not exist a second ago.
- Commuter (off panel): Geser ke dalem, Mas! Masih muat!

## Page 3 — Tidur Ayam
Layout `1-FULL`. Tone: the calm inside the crush.

**3.1 · `LOC-KRL` · 07:35 · Deerock, anonymous commuters**
The full page: a packed morning carriage, bodies pressed on every side. Deerock is wedged upright
among them, backpack squashed against his front, headphones on, eyes closing — his hands nowhere
near a strap, because the crowd is holding him up. Cool window daylight.
- Thought — Deerock: *Waktunya tidur ayam. 40 menit. Nggak bakal jatuh juga.*

## Page 5 — Perjuangan Layar Kecil
Layout `2-SIDE`, 5.1 about 60% of the width. Tone: a small, familiar suffering.

**5.1 · `LOC-KRL` · 07:50 · Deerock, anonymous commuters**
Deerock has worked one arm free and holds the phone close to his face, squinting behind his
glasses, the other arm still pinned. The carriage lurches; a stranger's shoulder presses into his,
someone's elbow in his side. Tense.
- Thought — Deerock: *Review ratusan baris kode… di layar HP… sambil terguncang…*

**5.2 · `LOC-KRL` · 07:55 · (phone screen)**
Seen over Deerock's shoulder, the phone fills the tall, narrow panel: a pull request diff, with
red and green rows of tiny, cramped code wrapping and spilling past the edges of the small screen.
Nothing is readable.
- Thought — Deerock: *Ini fungsi apa sih, panjang banget…*

## Continuity

- An ordinary weekday. The episode does not say whether it is the day after Episode 01, so it
  neither needs nor contradicts a follow-up to Mika's "Oh iya, besok…" message.
- The morning follows the routine the trailer established: lobby at 08:45, desk at 08:50. This
  day's train is at 07:30, a little earlier than the trailer's.
- Crowd density: the trailer's 07:48 carriage, on another day, was busy but had breathing room;
  this one is peak crush. Morning density on the line varies from day to day
  (`bible/story-world.md`), so both hold.
- Time adds up: 07:35 dozing off to 07:45 awake is the ten minutes of 6.1.
- Deerock's destination station is never shown — it has no reference sheet. The carriage and
  Sentra Lintas cover the whole commute.
- Separation clause applies on pages 6–8 (Deerock and Kai). Kai: no glasses, short cropped black
  hair, headphones around the neck. Deerock: glasses, purple-black hair covering the ears, no
  headphones on him in the office.
- No new locations: `LOC-STATION`, `LOC-KRL` and `LOC-OFFICE` (lobby and Deerock's desk).
