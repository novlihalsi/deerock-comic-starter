# Story World

Geography, named places and recurring objects. Every location that appears more than once must
be listed here **and** have a row in `references/registry.md`.

## Geography

The series is set in Greater Jakarta (Jabodetabek), not Jakarta city alone. Deerock lives in
**Cisauk, Tangerang** and commutes into the **Jakarta business district** by KRL Commuter on
the **Tanah Abang ↔ Rangkasbitung** line. That commute is the spine of a normal episode:
home → station → carriage → office, and the reverse in the evening.

### Desk assignments at NEXA
The open workspace sheet fixes one desk per character (Deerock, Aki, Kai, Rin, Mika). Desk
positions do not move between episodes. When a panel shows the workspace, keep the same desk
for the same person.

## Recurring objects

Laptop (MacBook), phone, tumbler, backpack or tote, over-ear headphones, sticky notes, lanyard
with access card, Figma canvas, code editor, QA checklist, planning notebook, coffee.

Character-specific carries are listed per character in `bible/character-bible.md` — they are an
identity cue, so do not swap them between characters.

## Recurring themes

"just one small change" · "works on my machine" · waiting for an API · QA edge cases ·
Figma vs implementation · meetings · commute · lunch · payday · weekend · small wins
