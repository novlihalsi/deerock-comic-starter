# Visual Style Bible

## Overall
Contemporary manga/anime slice-of-life with clean linework, restrained cel shading, soft cinematic lighting, subtle halftone/texture, realistic modern environments, and expressive but natural faces.

## Color
Muted cool neutrals with soft purple/blue accents for Deerock's visual identity. Warm indoor lights can contrast with cooler commute/exterior scenes.

## Camera
Mix:
- medium conversational shots
- over-the-shoulder work shots
- environmental wides
- occasional close-ups for reactions
- KRL crowd/environment shots
Avoid constant dramatic hero angles.

## Locations
### Deerock home
Small modern apartment/bedroom, tidy but lived-in, desk with laptop/monitor, coffee mug, practical furniture, morning light.

### KRL
Modern Jakarta commuter rail. Realistic carriage, standing/sitting passengers, poles, overhead handles, phone use. Keep crowd density believable.

### Office
Modern product/software office: desks, monitors, meeting rooms, plants, whiteboards, Figma/code/QA artifacts.

### Cafe
Contemporary Indonesian cafe, natural light, laptops, realistic seating and props.

## Typography
Panel dialogue should be readable, short, and natural. Use Indonesian for spoken dialogue; English is allowed for code, UI labels, role names, and common tech terms.

## Negative style constraints
No katana, ninja, samurai, fantasy armor, supernatural effects, action aura, glowing weapons, or cyberpunk transformation unless a future story explicitly changes canon.
